What we publish

Contract risk

Honeypots where sells are blocked, contracts where the owner can mint supply or move balances, taxes that can be raised after you buy.

Listings

New pairs on major venues, with the size of the market they land in and what happened to the last five comparable listings.

Unlocks

Upcoming supply unlocks in dollars, alongside the price behaviour around the last few unlocks of the same size.

What we don't publish

No price calls. No entry points. No guaranteed anything, and no paid coverage of a specific asset. If nothing clears the threshold on a given day, nothing goes out that day.

How it works

  1. Collect. Events are pulled from public on-chain data and exchange announcements.
  2. Filter. Each one has to clear a risk or size threshold before it is worth anyone's attention.
  3. Publish. Duplicates are dropped, then the alert goes to Threads and to your inbox.
