What this is

Hardflag publishes automated informational alerts about crypto contracts, exchange listings and token unlock schedules. It is a data feed. It is not a recommendation service, a broker, an exchange or an investment adviser, and it does not manage anyone's funds.

Not financial advice

Nothing published here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. You are responsible for your own decisions.

No guarantees

We make no representation about returns, prices or outcomes. When an alert mentions what happened around a past listing or unlock, it is describing history, not forecasting anything.

Accuracy

Data comes from public sources and can be delayed, incomplete or wrong. A risk flag describes a property observed in a contract at a point in time. It is not a verdict on a project, a team or an asset. Check anything that matters against the contract yourself.

Risk

Crypto assets are volatile and you can lose all of your money. Past behaviour of a market does not repeat on request.
