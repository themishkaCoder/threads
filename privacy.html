Privacy

Last updated

What we collect

Your email address, if you choose to give it. Nothing else. This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no tracking scripts.

Why

To send you alerts about contract risk, exchange listings and token unlocks, and occasionally to explain how a particular alert was put together.

Legal basis

Consent, under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. You give it when you submit the subscription form, and you can take it back at any time.

Who processes it

Email delivery runs on beehiiv. Their infrastructure may store data outside the European Economic Area under standard contractual clauses. Links in our emails may point to affiliate partners, who process data under their own policies.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe. After that your address is removed from the active list and kept only on a suppression record, so that we cannot email you again by accident.

What you can ask for

Access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and the right to object. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. For anything else, write to us.

Complaints

You can complain to your national data protection authority. In Slovakia that is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the new version goes up on this page with a new date.
