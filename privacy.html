Who we are

Chain Alerts publishes automated alerts about crypto contract risk, exchange listings and token unlocks. This policy explains what data we collect and why.

What we collect

Only your email address, and only if you choose to subscribe. We do not run analytics, advertising pixels or tracking scripts on this site. We do not set cookies.

Why we collect it

To send you periodic alerts and occasional context about the events we track. Nothing else. We do not sell, rent or share your address with third parties for their own marketing.

Legal basis

Consent, under Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR. You give it by submitting the subscription form, and you can withdraw it at any time.

Processors

Email delivery is handled by beehiiv. Their infrastructure may store data outside the European Economic Area under standard contractual clauses. Links in our emails and posts may point to affiliate partners, who process data under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe. After that, your address is removed from the active list and retained only in a suppression record so we do not email you again.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to processing. Every email contains a one-click unsubscribe link. For any other request, contact us at the address below.

Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Slovakia this is the Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic.
